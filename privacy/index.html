Vehicle Tracker Terms of use

Privacy policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Vehicle Tracker is published by Dave. This policy describes what the app collects, where it is stored, and who else receives it. Contact dave@davetyler.net with privacy questions or to ask for your account to be deleted.

Information you provide

  • Account details: email address, password if you create one, display name, and profile photo. You can also sign in with Apple or Google. Those providers share the identity they give the app, such as a name and email, when you choose that sign-in.
  • Vehicle records you enter: name, year, model, VIN, license plate, mileage, insurance, registration, service, fuel, charging, inspections, costs, and notes.
  • Photos and files you attach, including pictures of vehicles, trips, accidents, insurance cards, and registration.
  • Accident details you choose to record, which can include a place, and information about other people if you type it in.
  • Household invites: the email address of someone you share one vehicle with, and whether they can view or edit that vehicle. Your other vehicles stay private.

Information collected while you use the app

  • Trip location. While a trip is recording, the app collects GPS points, speed, distance, and duration, including when the phone is locked or the app is in the background. Those points are saved with the trip. You can mark a trip business or personal.
  • Car connection. The app can tell when the phone connects to car Bluetooth, CarPlay audio, or Android Auto, and when that connection drops, so it can ask you to start or end a trip. It does not read messages, contacts, or other data from the car, and it does not use AirTags.
  • Camera and photo library, only when you take or choose a photo.
  • Reminders scheduled on the phone for service, insurance, registration, inspection, and trip prompts. Vehicle Tracker does not operate a remote push service for those reminders.

What stays on your phone

Face ID, Touch ID, and other biometric checks are performed by your phone to unlock locked documents. Vehicle Tracker does not receive biometric templates. A document-lock PIN, if you set one, is stored on the device. A copy of your garage, the home screen widget, Live Activity, and the watch app use data already on the phone. GPS for a trip stays on the phone and is then saved with that trip.

Where your records are stored

Account data, vehicle records, photos, and files are stored with Supabase so you can open the same garage after you sign in again. Purchases are charged by Apple or Google. Vehicle Tracker does not receive your card number. RevenueCat receives your account id and the store transaction so the app can tell whether Garage is active, expired, or restored.

Recall checks

When you check recalls, the app sends the VIN to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) to identify the vehicle, then sends the decoded year, make, and model to NHTSA’s public recall API. Opening the NHTSA recall page in the browser sends the VIN to NHTSA as well.

Maps

If you open directions, the destination is handed to Apple Maps or Google Maps. That app’s own policy applies after it opens.

Who else can see your data

Vehicle Tracker does not sell your personal information and does not show ads. People you invite can see the single vehicle you shared with them, according to the view or edit permission you set. Supabase stores the records. Apple or Google process sign-in, if you use those buttons, and all subscription payments. RevenueCat records subscription status. NHTSA receives a VIN or year, make, and model only when you run a recall check.

How long it is kept

Records stay until you delete them in the app or you ask for the account to be deleted. You can export a JSON backup from Profile. Email dave@davetyler.net to delete your account. Deleting the account deletes the vehicles and records stored with it. A copy may remain in a backup for a short time until that backup expires. The phone may keep a local copy until you sign out or delete the app.

Children

Vehicle Tracker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
