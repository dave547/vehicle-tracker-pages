The service

Vehicle Tracker is a log for vehicles you choose to keep: service, fuel or charging, documents, and, on Garage, trips, household sharing, a sell packet, and mileage reports. It is a record you maintain. It is not insurance, legal, tax, safety, or repair advice. You are responsible for the accuracy of what you enter, including mileage you use for taxes.

Your account

You need an account to keep a garage. You are responsible for activity under that account. You can export a JSON backup from Profile. To delete the account and the records stored with it, email dave@davetyler.net.

Free

Free includes one vehicle you own, a service log, fuel or charging, and document lock. You can still view a vehicle someone else has shared with you. That shared vehicle does not count as your second vehicle.

Garage subscription

Garage is an auto-renewable subscription with two plans:

  • Garage Monthly (garage_monthly): one month, $4.99 in the United States.
  • Garage Yearly (garage_yearly): one year, $39.99 in the United States.

The price you are charged is the price shown on the paywall in the App Store or Google Play, which can differ outside the United States. Garage adds more than one vehicle, sharing a vehicle with your household, trips with business and personal miles, a sell-packet PDF, and mileage reports (CSV and PDF).

Payment is charged to your Apple ID or Google Play account when you confirm the purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. There is no free trial. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their rules.

Restore purchases is on Profile. Use the same store account you used to subscribe.

If Garage ends

A canceled or expired Garage can still view the garage that is already there and export a backup. It cannot add a vehicle, edit records, share, start a trip, or generate a sell packet or mileage report. Someone you shared a vehicle with can still view that vehicle, and cannot edit it, while your Garage is canceled.

Sharing

Sharing applies to the one vehicle you select. You choose view-only or edit. You should share only with people who may see that vehicle’s records.

You give the service permission to store and display that material so the app can function, including showing a shared vehicle to the people you invited.
